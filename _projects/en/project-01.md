I designed these puzzles back in 2023 for my girlfriend, whose name translates to 'Daisy'. 

Just like a sudoku, each <span style="color:#FF796C"> <b>row</b></span>, <span style="color:#CEA2FD"> <b>column</b></span> and <span style="color:#68B6E3"> <b>3×3 block</b></span> may only contain digits 1-9 once. However, this puzzle consists of 12 partially overlapping blocks, and not every row and column has to (or can) contain each digit.
